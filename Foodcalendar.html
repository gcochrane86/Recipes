Recipe Instructions

Miso Glazed Aubergine Tacos

with Wedges & Fresh Lettuce

⏱ 40 Mins 🌱 Veggie 🥗 2 of 5 a day
Ingredients (2 People)
Aubergine
2 chunks
Potatoes
1 small pack
Miso Paste
1 sachet
Soft Shell Tacos
6 pieces
Red Onion & Vinegar
1 onion / 1 sachet
Sesame Mayo
2 sachets
Step-by-Step Method
1 Get Prepped
Preheat oven to 200°C. Chop potatoes into 2cm wedges. Trim aubergine and chop into 2cm chunks. Spread onto separate baking trays, drizzle with oil, season potatoes with salt and pepper[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
2 Roast & Pickle the Onion
Roast wedges for 25–30 mins and aubergine for 20–25 mins[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). Halve and thinly slice red onion; mix half in a bowl with cider vinegar, a pinch of sugar, and salt[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Set aside to quick-pickle[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
3 Finish the Prep
Finely shred iceberg lettuce and chop coriander[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span). In a small bowl, whisk together miso paste, honey, soy sauce, sugar, and 1 tbsp water per person[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
4 Toast the Seeds
Toast sesame seeds dry in a frying pan on medium-high for 2–3 mins[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). Stir half into the miso sauce and the other half into the mayonnaise[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
5 Finish the Aubergine
Stir fry remaining onion in a pan with oil until soft (4–5 mins)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Add roasted aubergine and miso sauce; cook 3–5 mins until sticky[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span). Warm tortillas in the oven for 2–3 mins[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
6 Assemble & Serve
Spread sesame mayo onto warm tortillas[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). Layer shredded lettuce, lime juice, sticky glazed aubergine, quick-pickled onion, and coriander[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span). Serve with golden wedges on the side[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
